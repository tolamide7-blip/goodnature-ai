Overview

GoodNature.ai ("we", "us") operates https://www.goodnature.ai from Austin, Texas, USA. We respect your privacy and minimize data collection. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can control it.

What data we collect

  • Contact form data: When you use our contact form at /contact.html, we receive your name, email, subject, and message via FormSubmit.co to forward it to hello@idwol.com. We keep this only to reply to you.
  • Server logs: Vercel hosting may log IP address, user agent, and pages visited for security and performance. These logs are short-term.
  • Analytics (if enabled): We may use privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics to see which guides are popular. No personal tracking for ads.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not sell your personal data
  • We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site ad tracking
  • We do not build profiles for ad targeting
  • We do not participate in private blog networks or link schemes that track you

Cookies

See our Cookie Policy for details. Essential cookies are needed for the site to work. You can block non-essential cookies in your browser.

How we use your data

  • To reply to your questions, corrections, or suggestions submitted via contact form
  • To maintain site security and fix bugs
  • To improve our guides based on aggregate usage

Data sharing

We share contact form submissions only with FormSubmit.co (to deliver the email) and Vercel (hosting provider) for technical delivery. We do not share with advertisers.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to processing of your personal data. To exercise these rights, email hello@idwol.com with the subject "Privacy Request". We will respond within 30 days.

Children

Our site is for general audiences and not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Data retention

Contact form messages are kept for up to 12 months to handle follow-ups, then deleted unless legal retention is required.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version with an updated date and, if significant, note it on the homepage.

Contact
