Our mission

GoodNature.ai helps you clean more simply. We write practical, safe, low-waste guidance for real homes — small apartments, busy families, and anyone who doesn't want 15 different sprays under the sink.

We started GoodNature.ai because most cleaning advice online is too complicated, too product-heavy, or unsafe. We focus on using fewer products, testing on small areas first, and knowing when to stop and call a professional.

What makes us different

  • Tested in real homes: Every method is tried in a real 2-bedroom apartment in Austin, Texas before we publish. We note what fails, not just what works.
  • Safety first: We never recommend mixing bleach with vinegar, ammonia, or rubbing alcohol. We highlight surface risks (e.g., vinegar can etch natural stone and marble).
  • No fluff: Plain English, short steps, and clear warnings. We do not use keyword stuffing or publish auto-generated content at scale without human review.

How we create content (E-E-A-T)

Learn more about our process on our Editorial Policy and Author pages:

  • Experience: Real-world testing in small homes with limited storage.
  • Expertise: Researching product labels, EPA Safer Choice, and manufacturer instructions.
  • Authoritativeness: Each guide has a last-reviewed date and links to corrections.
  • Trustworthiness: Same content for users and crawlers (no cloaking), no bought links, working contact form.

What we don't do

  • No medical cleaning advice
  • No instructions for hazardous chemical mixing
  • No scraping or re-publishing other sites' content
  • No link schemes or private blog networks
