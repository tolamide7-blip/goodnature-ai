Corrections Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026 | Contact: hello@idwol.com | Location: Austin, Texas, USA

Our commitment to accuracy

At GoodNature.ai, based in Austin, Texas, we strive to provide accurate, safe, and practical household cleaning guidance. Every guide is human-reviewed, tested in a real home where possible, and checked for safety risks before publication. See our Editorial Policy for our full review process.

What we correct

We correct:

  • Factual errors (e.g., incorrect dilution ratios, wrong surface compatibility)
  • Safety omissions (e.g., missing warning about not mixing bleach with vinegar, ammonia, or rubbing alcohol)
  • Clarity issues that could lead to misuse
  • Broken links or outdated product information

How we handle corrections

  1. Report: Email hello@idwol.com or use our Contact page with subject "Correction". Include the URL, what is wrong, and your suggested fix or source if you have one.
  2. Review: Our editorial team reviews the report within 2-3 business days, checks product labels, manufacturer instructions, and safety sheets.
  3. Fix: If confirmed, we update the article promptly.
  4. Disclosure: For significant factual or safety corrections, we add a note at the bottom of the article: "Corrected on [Date]: [What was changed]"

Updates vs. corrections

We review popular guides every 6 months to ensure they remain accurate and safe. Minor updates for clarity, formatting, or adding a helpful tip are not listed as corrections. Only factual or safety-related changes receive a formal correction note.

Transparency

We do not hide corrections. We do not use cloaking — we show the same corrected content to users and to search crawlers (Bingbot, Googlebot). We do not buy links or participate in link schemes to hide errors.
