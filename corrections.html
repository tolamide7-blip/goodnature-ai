Corrections

Accuracy and responsible household guidance matter to us. If you find a factual error, broken link, unsafe wording, accessibility issue or unclear statement, please contact the editorial team.

How to report an issue

Email hello@idwol.com with the article URL, the sentence or section concerned, and a short explanation of the suggested correction.

Review process

We assess reports, verify the relevant information and update the page when a correction is appropriate. Significant changes may include an updated date and an editorial note when useful.

Safety reports

If a page appears to recommend mixing cleaning products or could create an immediate safety risk, identify the page clearly in your message and do not attempt the method.
