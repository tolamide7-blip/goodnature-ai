What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. GoodNature.ai uses cookies to make our site work and to understand how it is used.

How we use cookies

  • Essential cookies: Required for the site to function (e.g., page navigation). These cannot be switched off.
  • Analytics cookies: If enabled, we use privacy-friendly analytics to understand which guides are helpful (e.g., page views). We do not sell your data.
  • Preference cookies: Remember your choices where applicable.

We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking for ad targeting.

Third-party cookies

Our contact form uses FormSubmit.co to deliver messages securely. When you submit the contact form, FormSubmit processes your name, email, and message to forward it to us at hello@idwol.com. Please see FormSubmit's privacy policy for details. We also use Vercel for hosting, which may use essential cookies for security and performance.

How to control cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings
  • Use your browser's private/incognito mode
  • For more information, visit allaboutcookies.org

If you block essential cookies, parts of the site may not work properly (e.g., form submission).

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, delete, or object to certain data processing. Contact us at hello@idwol.com or via our Contact page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. We will post the new version with an updated "Last updated" date.
